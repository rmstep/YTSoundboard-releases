YTSoundboard

How this works. A share link doesn't contain any audio. It's a recipe: the YouTube video, the start and end times, and a title. YTSoundboard re-cuts each sound on your own computer, so nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere, and everything after the “#” in this address stays in your browser.

Previews are played by YouTube's own player. Only clip audio you have the right to use. YTSoundboard is an independent project and isn't affiliated with YouTube.

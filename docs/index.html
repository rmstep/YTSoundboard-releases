What is YTSoundboard?

YTSoundboard is a YouTube soundboard for Windows. You pick a start and end time on any YouTube video with the Chrome extension, and the clip becomes a sound in the app. Press its hotkey and everyone in your Discord call, stream or game hears it through your microphone, while you hear it through your speakers.

Clip from YouTube

Drag a start and end on the video. The extension even suggests the most replayed 7 seconds.

Global hotkeys

Trigger sounds with a key combination while a game or call is in focus.

Plays into your mic

Mixes your real mic and your sounds into a virtual microphone for Discord, OBS, Zoom and games.

Stream Deck

A plugin puts each sound on a key, with its name and thumbnail.

Get started

  1. Download and run the installer.
  2. When asked, install the free virtual microphone (VB-Cable). The app downloads it from VB-Audio and checks its signature.
  3. In Discord, OBS or your game, choose CABLE Output as your microphone.
  4. Add the YTSoundboard Clipper extension to Chrome (or Edge).
  5. Open a YouTube video, click YTSoundboard, pick your clip, press a hotkey, and add it.

Frequently asked questions

Is YTSoundboard free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT license.

Does it work with Discord, OBS and games?

Yes. Choose “CABLE Output” as the microphone in any app. People hear your normal voice plus the sounds you trigger.

Why does it need a virtual microphone?

Windows cannot send audio into a microphone without a virtual audio device. YTSoundboard uses the free VB-Cable driver for this and offers to install it for you.

Does it work with Stream Deck?

Yes. Click “Install Stream Deck plugin” in the app, then drag “Play Sound” onto a key.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. YTSoundboard is Windows only.

Is my data collected?

No. Everything stays on your computer. Read the privacy policy.

Is it affiliated with YouTube?

No. YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC. YTSoundboard is an independent project. Only clip audio you have the right to use.
